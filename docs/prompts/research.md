# Prompt — reusable research dossier

Follow `AGENTS.md`, `docs/agents/research-and-evidence.md`, and `research-scout`.

**Question or topic:** [GOAL]
**Possible application (may be unknown):** [CONSTRAINTS]

Search local research first and avoid duplicate surveys. Investigate broadly enough to compare credible alternatives, but bound the work to a usable conclusion. Separate primary sources, secondary commentary, observations, hypotheses and unanswered questions. Preserve dates, citations, licenses/provenance and any disconfirming evidence. If the project connection is speculative, record it as exploratory; do not force relevance or promote an idea to approved scope.

Deliver a concise, indexed research record with findings, confidence, practical examples, possible cross-project reuses, risks, and conditions under which the finding would be useful. Propose optional experiments or decisions separately. No implementation, external writes or automatic product-policy changes unless explicitly requested.
